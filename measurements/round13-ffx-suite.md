## Plain summary

1. **AMD's FidelityFX suite splits cleanly into four closed machine-learning DLLs and a larger set of open MIT compute effects (`READ`).** FSR SDK v2.4.0 ("Redstone", 2026-10-28) ships pre-built binary-only DLLs for FSR Upscaling 4.1.2, ML Frame Generation 4.0.2, Ray Regeneration 1.2.1, and Radiance Caching 0.9.0 Preview alongside open MIT analytic FSR 2/3 and Frame Generation 3.1.6; classic FidelityFX SDK v1.1.4 (2025-05-08) ships 16 open MIT effects. Under this study's rules, only MIT shaders are inspected or compiled; the closed ML DLLs are measured strictly from the outside.
2. **AMD's open analytic frame generation compiles to zero FP16 instructions (`READ` + `MEASURED`).** Across all 18 passes of FSR 3.1 Optical Flow and Frame Interpolation (`76` compiled RADV/ACO pipelines), FP16 share is **`0.0%`** even when forcibly compiled with `-DFFX_HALF=1`. Both `*_shaderblobs.cpp` files ignore `is16bit` in every pass, and the three pyramid shaders explicitly turn off `FFX_HALF`. Classic MIT filters (`CAS`, `FSR 1`, `LPM`, `Denoiser` reflections, `CACAO` blur, `SPD`) pack heavily into `v_pk_*_f16` (`19.6–72.7%`), while `FSR 3.1` upscaling stays mostly FP32 (`7.9–8.8%` pure FP16 in `accumulate` only).
3. **`v_msad_u8` and `v_sad_*` run at full vector rate with zero FMA co-issue in both wave32 and wave64 (`MEASURED` + `READ`).** AMD's Vulkan GLSL optical-flow header omits `msad4`, compiling `optical_flow_v5` to `1,365–1,372` INT32 instructions (`78.1%`) instead of hardware `v_msad_u8`. On hardware (Slot 2 bank-free `msad_w32` and `msad_w64`, both **`PASS`** with breaks failing), all four SAD/MSAD ops are bit-exact (`0` errors across `8,192` w32 + `16,384` w64 vectors), have **`5.000`-cycle** dependent latency, issue at full 32-lane vector rate (**`1.000` cyc/op** wave32, **`2.000` cyc/op** wave64 — not quarter-rate like `v_mul_lo_u32`), and never co-issue with FP32 FMA (`1+1 = 2.000`, `1+3 = 4.000` w32; `2+1 = 3.000`, `2+3 = 5.000` w64).
4. **Post-review texture sampling and FP16-beside-texture reruns passed cleanly, confirming `~4 TMU/CU` L0 filtering, double-rate `texelFetch`, `90.7–98.1%` GDDR6 saturation, hardware DCC on structured floats, and full VALU hiding behind DRAM (`vk_tex`, `vk_tex_fp16`, Slot 5, `MEASURED` + `INFERRED`).** Earlier Slot 4 runs are withdrawn after code-review fixes (`TOP_OF_PIPE` dispatch start timestamps, coherent end-clock ordering, odd-parity DRAM ALU exactness, and hard `< 60 C` cooldowns; `0` `INSUFFICIENT_CLK`, breaks failed, `0` faults). On this overclocked, unpinned-clock card with a raised power limit: at L0 (`16 KiB`), `1–8 B` `textureLod` (`3.50–4.11 samp/clk/CU`, `690–773 GSamp/s`) and `textureGather` (`3.54–4.27`, `691–763 GSamp/s`) hit **`~4.0 TMU/CU`** with **`RGBA16F` full-rate**, while unfiltered `texelFetch` (`image_load`) reached **`6.19–7.46 /clk/CU`** (`1,238–1,430 GSamp/s`, **`11.01–11.04 TB/s`** on `RGBA16F`); `RGBA32F` halved (`3.54/3.11` fetch, `1.92/1.89` lod) and single-channel `textureGather` recovered **`1.88–1.93x`**. On visit-once `512 MiB` (`256 MiB` `R8`) DRAM at `1408 MHz` UCLK (theoretical bus peak **`720.90 GB/s`**), incompressible `RGBA8` (`660.0–707.1 GB/s`), `B10G11R11` (`654.1–707.5 GB/s`), and `R8` (`texelFetch` `700.0–705.6 GB/s`) reached **`90.7–98.1%`** of peak, whereas 7-bit dyadic float textures exceeded `720.9 GB/s` in most cells (`R16G16F` `786.9–806.4` with `textureLod`/`textureGather` w32 below at `692.7`/`705.3`; `RGBA16F` `746.1–804.6` with `textureGather` w32 below at `714.5`; `RGBA32F` `833.3–1,159.8 GB/s` in all 6 cells; full table in §6.2), isolating (`INFERRED`) hardware DCC on `VK_IMAGE_TILING_OPTIMAL`. Beside texture traffic (`vk_tex_fp16`), pure ALU hit `50.0–51.2 TFLOPS` (`60.9–62.3 inst/clk/CU`) for `v_pk_fma_f16` and `49.6 TFLOPS` (`120.8 inst/clk/CU`, `1.92x` w32) for wave64 `fp32`, and DRAM fetches (`535.6–581.7 GB/s` `RGBA8`, `769.6–798.7 GB/s` `RGBA16F`) hid all **`N = 64` FMAs/tap (`vs_max = 0.999–1.053`)**. Caveats: clocks were unpinned (`95/108` `vk_tex` and `109/112` `vk_tex_fp16` L0/L2/MALL cells tripped `SPREAD>10%`, and two `vk_tex_fp16` `N=0` clock drops pulled `vs_max` to `0.63–0.78`), and `uclk` sat at `96 MHz` on `102/108` `vk_tex` and `110/112` `vk_tex_fp16` non-DRAM cells (which can affect `tex` cache-spill/MALL traffic, whereas every DRAM cell ran at `1408 MHz`).
5. **Equal-work all-Vulkan async compute reruns reproduce severe `N = 80` chained-dispatch starvation beside saturated graphics, with unpinned-clock and thermal caveats (`vk_async`, Slot 7, `MEASURED` + `INFERRED`).** Earlier Slot 3 (`chain=40`) results are withdrawn after code-review fixes restoring equal-work `N = 1` vs `N = 80` SAT chains; two intermediate runs (`slot5` `vk_async` and `slot6`) were `90 C` thermal stops, not results. With the owner-approved `95 C` hotspot stop, Slot 7 completed all `64/64` overlap cells exact (`bad=0`, `--break` failed `4/4`, peak `90 C`, `0` faults). RADV exposes a dedicated compute-only queue family (`qf=1`, `count=4`), and unprivileged `VK_KHR_global_priority` allows `LOW` and `MEDIUM` while refusing `HIGH` and `REALTIME` (`VK_ERROR_NOT_PERMITTED`). Under saturated graphics (`SAT-100`, overclocked card, unpinned clocks), equal-work `N = 80` chains hit the 33-frame graphics burst cap on all `16/16` cells (`comp_strict_ov = 0/2`, **`119.6–133.3 ms`**, **`10.78–37.27x`** vs alone, hotspot **`87–90 C`**) across all four workloads (`fp16`, `sad`, `tex`, `stream`) at both `LOW` and `MEDIUM` priority while graphics cadence stayed near baseline (`3.52–3.76 ms`, `1.05–1.12x`), mirroring Round 12's HIP `chain=80` starvation ([blindspot-followups.md](blindspot-followups.md) §2) and pointing (`INFERRED`) to hardware/firmware queue arbitration. By contrast, monolithic `N = 1` chains finished in `9.7–27.0 ms` (`1.96–7.94x`, `2/2` strict overlap) while slowing graphics to `5.37–7.19 ms` (`1.60–2.14x`). **Clock caveats:** unpinned SCLK sat near `1.9 GHz` (`1884–2231 MHz`) on `SAT-100 N=1` arms vs `3.2 GHz` (`3209–3223 MHz`) on `N=80` arms (so `slow_vs_alone` mixes clock state with starvation), and `uclk` sat at `96 MHz` on `5/8` compute-alone lines and `46/64` overlap arms (`stream` dropped from `1408 MHz` alone to `434–875 MHz` overlapped, directly affecting `stream` and `tex`). Under `PACE-60`, `N = 80` ran `1.19–1.52x` (`fp16`/`sad`), `0.98–3.27x` (`tex`, `uclk=96 MHz`), and `2.24–2.38x` (`stream`, `uclk=875 MHz`).
6. **Uncapped Ray Regeneration 1.2.0 matched AMD's published timing; Radiance Caching 0.9.0 ran cleanly on WMMA while non-WMMA still failed pipeline creation (`MEASURED` + `READ`).** Switching headless gamescope to `MAILBOX` + `dxgi.syncInterval = 0` + `-r 1000` uncapped Ray Regeneration 1.2.0 (`1920x1080` display / `960x540` render) to **`3.058 ms/frame` (`327.0 FPS`, `100%` busy, `3165 MHz` SCLK, `71 C`)**, with **`FSR Ray Regeneration = 1.140 ms`** (vs AMD's `0.99 + 0.13 = 1.12 ms`), `Pathtracing = 1.142 ms`, and `FSR Upscaling 4.1.1 = 0.337 ms`. Radiance Caching 0.9.0 (`1280x720`) reproduced WMMA at **`3.750 ms/frame` (`266.7 FPS`, `100%` busy, `3422 MHz`, `80 C`)**, whereas `nowmma` still failed Vulkan compute pipeline creation (`0` presents) even with `dxgi.hideAmdGpu = True` + `amdxc64=d`.
7. **Analytic Upscaler `3.1.6` is `1.58–1.88x` faster than FSR `4.1.2` FP8; Cauldron has an `fgoff` `m_StartTime` bug; and ML Frame Generation `4.0.2` ran in a disposable Windows 11 prefix (`MEASURED` + `READ` + `INFERRED`).** Hiding AMD GPU + `amdxc64=d` + coopmat/FP8 cleanly switched Upscaler to `3.1.6` (`0.218 / 0.370 / 0.885 ms` vs `4.1.2` FP8 `0.344 / 0.650 / 1.661 ms` vs Slot 1 `4.1.2` no-FP8 `0.695 / 1.088 / 2.525 ms`). **`FidelityFX_FSR.exe` resets `m_PerfFrameCount` without resetting `m_StartTime` when `F7` toggles FG off (`framework.cpp:1726-1750`)**, inflating `fgoff`'s `1000 / AvgFPS` by `~1.7–2.0 ms` in Slot 1, Slot 2, and the ML FG follow-up. *Dated Slot 2 note (Win10 prefix):* both Slot 2 arms reported identical FG VRAM (`105.75 / 183.44 / 392.19 MB` total; `67.8 / 117.2 / 249.9 MB` persistent) and `VKD3D_QUEUE_PROFILE` async compute submits (`0.300–0.311 / 0.510–0.516 / 1.075–1.095 ms`, `2.00x` presents, `0.0%` median overlap), leaving the FG version unproven at the time (§6.6). In the Win11 follow-up (`mlfg/compare-run1/`, `30` cells, `0` faults, §6.7), the ML path ran in a disposable copy of the game prefix set to report Windows 11 (setup steps not published), activating ML FG `4.0.2` (`INFERRED` from combined outside-view signals after a post-trial proof-gate widening): async compute `0x021c` p50 rose from Analytic `3.1.6`'s **`0.320 / 0.548 / 1.127 ms`** to **`0.548 / 0.834 / 1.546 ms`** (`1.71x / 1.52x / 1.37x` at `3170–3199 MHz` SCLK), `FG Prepare` roughly doubled (`1.6–2.0x`), `GetWaveMatrixProperties` rose from `0` to `25` calls, and persistent FG VRAM dropped from **`67.8 / 117.2 / 249.9 MB`** (`36%` aliasable) to **`35.1 / 43.3 / 64.2 MB`** (`60–65%` aliasable).

### 2B. Four structural takeaways from compiled MIT code (`READ` + `MEASURED`)

1. **Analytic Frame Generation is 0% FP16 (`READ` + `MEASURED`):** Both v2.4.0 (`r13-ffx/inventory/INVENTORY.md:113-128`) and v1.1.4 ([round13-mit-stats.md](round13-mit-stats.md)) confirm that `ALLOW_FP16` is dead plumbing across all 18 analytic Optical Flow + Frame Interpolation passes. Every pass getter leaves `is16bit` unnamed/unused, all three SPD pyramid shaders explicitly undefine `FFX_HALF`, and compiling with `-DFFX_HALF=1` produces byte-for-byte identical ACO pipeline hashes and instruction counts. Sixteen-bit types appear only in internal texture formats (`R16G16B16A16_FLOAT`, `R16G16_FLOAT`, `R16G16_SINT`) that hardware loads unpack into 32-bit registers.
2. **FSR 3.1 Upscaler is mostly FP32 on PC/Vulkan (`READ` + `MEASURED`):** Only `accumulate` / `accumulate_sharpen` executes FP16 arithmetic (`68` `v_pk_*_f16` + `70–72` scalar `*_f16` = `7.9–8.8%`, plus `92` `v_fma_mix_f32` = `5.2%` in w64), while pure FP32 stays `50.6–56.7%`. `CMakeCompileFSR3UpscalerShaders.txt:26-30` disables `DATA_HALF` for upsample, accumulate, and lock-status samplers (`# Only reprojection is to do half for now`), `ffx_fsr3upscaler_rcas.h:28` gates 16-bit RCAS behind `__XBOX_SCARLETT`, and the other three `_16bit`-selected passes (`shading_change`, `prepare_reactivity`, `autogen_reactive`) compile to `0.0%` FP16 VALU.
3. **Vulkan GLSL Optical Flow omits `msad4`, whereas HLSL/DX12 uses it (`READ` + `MEASURED`):** In `sdk/include/FidelityFX/gpu/opticalflow/ffx_opticalflow_callbacks_glsl.h:28-29`, AMD sets `#define FFX_OPTICALFLOW_USE_MSAD4_INSTRUCTION 0` (`// no msad4 in glsl`). Consequently, `optical_flow_v5` compiles on Vulkan to `0` `v_msad_u8` and `1,365–1,372` INT32 instructions (`78.1%`, software packed-byte SAD using `v_sub_nc_u32`, `v_max_i32`, `v_subrev_nc_u32`, `v_bfe_u32`, `v_add3_u32`), whereas the HLSL/DX12 source (`ffx_opticalflow_compute_optical_flow_v5.h:114-136`) emits `16` `msad4` calls per inner loop.
4. **Wave32 VOPD vs. Wave64 VGPR halving (`MEASURED`):** Across all `364` pipelines, ACO emits `v_dual_*` (VOPD dual-issue) **only in Wave32** (`0` VOPD lines across all `182` Wave64 pipelines; up to `392` in `fsr1__easu__fp32__w32`, `298` in `fsr3upscaler__shading_change_pyramid__fp32__w32`, `277` in `fsr3upscaler__accumulate__fp32__w32`). Conversely, Wave64 frequently halves VGPRs (`96 -> 48` on `optical_flow_v5`, `fsr1__rcas`, `cas`, `parallelsort__scatter`; `96 -> 72` on `fsr3upscaler__accumulate__fp16`).

### 6.6 Analytic Upscaler 3.1.6, Frame Generation comparison, Cauldron's `fgoff` timing bug, and `VKD3D_QUEUE_PROFILE` (`MEASURED` + `READ` + `INFERRED`)

Ran `30` cells (`24` ABBA + `6` `VKD3D_QUEUE_PROFILE`, `UCLK = 1408 MHz`, `SCLK = 3359–3518 MHz`, `HotMax <= 77 C`, `0` faults; `slot2-20261127T061718/fg/`).

#### 1. Why `fgon` showed LOWER `1000 / AvgFPS` than `fgoff` (Cauldron `m_StartTime` bug — `READ` + `MEASURED`)
In `10` of `12` Slot 2 ABBA pairs, `1000 / AvgFPS` reported a **lower** frame time with FG ON than with FG OFF (e.g., `1080p fg402`: `4.665 ms` ON vs `4.900 ms` OFF). Reading Cauldron's own benchmark code (`sample-src/framework.cpp:524, 607-608, 1701-1750, 1888-1895`) explains this completely:
- `framework.cpp:1706` records `m_StartTime = steady_clock::now()` once when content finishes loading (`commencing benchmark`).
- In every `fgoff` cell (`n7=1`), `run_fg_compare.sh` waits `KEY_WAIT = 4 s` (`~2–3 s` **after** `commencing benchmark`, confirmed in `Cauldron.log`: e.g., `06:19:16 -> 06:19:19` and `06:25:05 -> 06:25:08`) and presses `F7` to turn FG off.
- Removing the `FG Prepare` GPU marker hits `framework.cpp:1726-1750` (`Timing markers changed during benchmark. Resetting stats.`), which clears `m_CpuPerfStats` / `m_GpuPerfStats` and resets `m_PerfFrameCount = 1`, **without resetting `m_StartTime`**.
- At shutdown (`framework.cpp:524, 607-608`), `Runtime = now - m_StartTime` and `AvgFPS = m_PerfFrameCount / Runtime`. Every `fgoff` cell therefore folds `~2–3 s` of pre-`F7` wall time into `Runtime` while dividing by only `1,501` post-`F7` frames — inflating `fgoff` `1000 / AvgFPS` by **`~+1.7 to +2.0 ms/frame`** (both in Slot 2 and Slot 1!).
